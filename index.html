MEMORANDUM FOR ARTI GILL SEPTEMBER 2026

Arti —

In building and scaling consumer wellness brands in India, the most insurmountable barrier is rarely marketing — it is identifying a category with daily, non-discretionary repeat necessity where the chemical formulations, clinical trials, and manufacturing supply chain are already rock-solid.

“Pain relief patches are the single most under-built brand category in Indian consumer health — high margin, recurring daily demand, and ripe for D2C/q-commerce brand dominance.”

As Indian consumers shift decisively away from gastric-eroding oral NSAIDs and messy, foul-smelling balms, transdermal herbal patches provide instant, localized, 12-hour relief with zero stomach toxicity.

Inside this monograph, we present the investment thesis for acquiring the Viopatch® brand into the Giga Capital ecosystem. Investment Rationalization GIGA CAPITAL SYNERGY

Why this fits an early-stage brand-incubation thesis.

1. A Brand with the Hard Parts Already Solved

Formulation, clinical validation, stability testing, manufacturing relationship, and multi-country regulatory clearances exist today. Acquisition buys years of derisked pharmaceutical R&D, not an unproven conceptual MVP.

2. Recurring-Demand Category, Not a Consumer Fad

Pain management is chronic, recurrent, and inescapable across age brackets (geriatric osteoarthritis, white-collar posture pain, postpartum and menstrual aches). This is the exact opposite of a hit-driven or trend-vulnerable consumer bet.

3. Clear Value-Creation Levers Post-Acquisition

The brand has thrived primarily on organic pharmacy pull and basic Amazon listings. A modern D2C playbook — 10-minute quick-commerce penetration (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart), persona-targeted creative campaigns, and high-margin subscription bundles — can unlock immediate 5x scale.
