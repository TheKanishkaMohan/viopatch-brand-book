## Executive Summary

This Brand Style Guide establishes the permanent, single source of truth for **Viopatch**, India's pioneer transdermal herbal pain relief patch brand, manufactured by **Unexo Life Sciences Pvt. Ltd.** (established 1972).

Incorporating all empirical diagnostics, packaging evolutions, review comments, and corporate governance standards codified in the official presentation (`VIOPATCH_BRAND_STYLE_GUIDE.pptx`) and master template (`VIOPATCH-PRESENTATION-TEMPLATE.potx`), this specification:
1. Articulates the **Stepped Value Hierarchy Pyramid** anchored by the North Star Purpose of **Active Freedom**.
2. Establishes the **Dual-Color Master Wordmark**, inviolable 3.7:1 aspect ratio, strict 1X clear space boundary, and cap-height registered trademark (®).
3. Codifies the **8 Inviolable Prohibitions** for brand logotype integrity.
4. Harmonizes the corporate palette under the 8 approved brand colors: **Viopatch Cool Blue (`#0054A6`)** (the single, unified blue standard across all touchpoints), **Viopatch Dermal Yellow (`#FDCD8B`)** (warm dermal substrate & VIO wordmark), **Viopatch Herbal Green (`#379131`)**, **Viopatch Thermal Orange (`#F26B43`)**, **Viopatch Menthol Frost (`#0284C7`)**, **Viopatch Clinical Slate (`#475569`)**, **Viopatch Statutory Charcoal (`#2A2A28`)**, and **Viopatch Hospital Calm Grey (`#F2F2F7`)**.
5. Implements the **Cross-Platform Two-Tier Typography Architecture** (Tier 1 Industrial Print packaging press plates + Tier 2 Digital Google Fonts stack with embedded TrueType binaries).
6. Defines the **2026 Refreshed Packaging 4-Zone Architecture** across planar 2D elevations and isometric 3D cartons and pouches.
7. Documents the synchronized **PowerPoint Slide Master & Template Design System** (`VIOPATCH_BRAND_STYLE_GUIDE.pptx` and `VIOPATCH-PRESENTATION-TEMPLATE.potx`) with strict 1X logo clearance on all title placeholders, custom layouts, and pre-configured brand theme palettes.
8. Codifies how **Approved Font Weights** are structurally enforced via PowerPoint master styles, embedded TrueType subsets, and layout reset governance.

## 2. Logo Anatomy, Clear Space & Size Standards

### 2.1 The Two-Tone Wordmark Specification
The Viopatch logo is a bespoke dual-color typographic wordmark set in ultra-bold condensed geometric letterforms:
- **`VIO`**: Rendered in **Viopatch Indian Skin** (`#FDCD8B` / PMS 1345 C), symbolizing human skin, transdermal biocompatibility, and anatomical warmth.
- **`PATCH`**: Rendered in **Viopatch Blue** (`#0054A6` / PMS 2145 C) on white and light backgrounds, or **Pure White** (`#FFFFFF`) on dark navy backgrounds.
- **Registered Trademark (`®`)**: Mandatory glyph locked at cap-height immediately following the terminal letter `H`.

### 2.2 Geometry & Aspect Ratio Lock
- **Inviolable Aspect Ratio**: The official master logotype has a permanent, locked aspect ratio of **3.7:1** (Width to Height).
- **Prohibition on Deformation**: Horizontal stretching, vertical squashing, condensed forced-tracking, or italicized shearing is strictly prohibited across all media.

### 2.3 Clear Space Boundary ($1X$) & Presentation Template Enforcement
- **The $1X$ Rule**: Maintain an inviolable exclusion perimeter equal to **$1X$** (the full vertical height $H$ of the logotype wordmark) on all four sides.
- **Presentation Title Clearance**: In PowerPoint presentations and templates, the upper-right master logo sits at `Left = 808 pt`, `Top = 28 pt`, `Width = 94.4 pt`, `Height = 25 pt`. The 1X clear space boundary requires a minimum exclusion distance of $25\text{ pt}$ to the left of the logo:
  $$\text{Maximum Allowed Title Right Edge} = 808\text{ pt} - 25\text{ pt} = 783\text{ pt}$$
- **Template Enforcement**: All Title Placeholders across `slideMaster1.xml` and all 21 custom layouts (`slideLayout*.xml`) are permanently capped to a maximum width of **720 pt** (`Left = 43.2 pt` or `57.6 pt`, ending at $\le 777.6\text{ pt}$). This guarantees $> 30\text{ pt}$ ($> 1.2\text{X}$) of clear space, strictly prohibiting headlines (e.g., Slide 4) from encroaching upon or overlapping the logo.

### 2.4 The Four Balanced Brand Anchors (Slide 3 Standard)
1. **1X Clear Space**: Height of logo ($H$) boundary on all 4 sides; no typography, borders, or icons may intrude.
2. **Locked Geometry**: Inviolable 3.7:1 aspect ratio; zero horizontal stretching or vertical squashing.
3. **Statutory Trademark (®)**: Registered glyph locked at cap-height beside `H`; mandatory on all commercial packaging and digital touchpoints.
4. **Minimum Reproduction**: Print: 25 mm width | Digital: 120 px width (240 px on Retina / 2x screens).

## 4. Prohibited Logotype Usages (8 Inviolable Violations)

| # | Prohibited Action | Enforcement Rule | Rationale |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **✕ Do Not Distort** | Never stretch horizontally or squash vertically. The **3.7:1** aspect ratio must remain permanently locked. | Optical deformation destroys wordmark geometry and weakens trademark distinctiveness. |
| **2** | **✕ Do Not Recolor** | Never alter brand hues. `VIO` is strictly Skin (`#FDCD8B`) and `PATCH` is strictly Blue (`#0054A6`) or White (`#FFFFFF`). | Off-brand colors dilute brand equity and cause consumer confusion. |
| **3** | **✕ Do Not Outline** | Never stroke, outline, or wireframe the letterforms (*IBM Brand Rule*). Letterforms must always be solid fills. | Outlines break letter weight consistency and degrade at small reproduction sizes. |
| **4** | **✕ Do Not Add Shadows** | Never apply drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, embossing, or 3D extrusions (*Uber Brand Rule*). Wordmark must remain crisp and flat. | Decorative skeuomorphic effects create visual noise and look amateurish. |
| **5** | **✕ Do Not Omit ®** | Never omit, shrink below legibility, or relocate the registered trademark symbol (`®`). Must remain locked at cap-height beside `H`. | Statutory requirement to preserve registered trademark protection under Indian & international IP law. |
| **6** | **✕ Do Not Rotate or Tilt** | Never rotate or place the logotype on an angle or diagonal layout (*Uber Brand Rule*). Always align horizontally at 0°. | Angled presentation reduces reading velocity and violates clinical authority standards. |
| **7** | **✕ Do Not Violate Spacing** | Never crowd inside tight borders, cards, or busy graphic containers (*IBM Brand Rule*). Maintain the **1X clear space** perimeter. | Visual crowding impairs instant shelf pop and compromises brand stature. |
| **8** | **✕ Do Not Use Low Contrast** | Never place the blue wordmark on dark navy, black, or complex photographic backgrounds (*Uber Brand Rule*). Always use the white-text variant on dark backgrounds. | Inadequate contrast fails ISO/IEC legibility and accessibility standards. |

### 8.4 How Font "Approved Weights" Are Enforced via Templates
In Microsoft PowerPoint and Office OpenXML templates (`.potx`), font families and weights are governed across three distinct architectural layers:

1. **Master & Layout Placeholder Default Styles (`<p:txStyles>`)**:
   - In `slideMaster1.xml` and each `slideLayout*.xml`, every text placeholder level declares default run formatting (`<a:defRPr>`) where `b="1"` (Bold) or `b="0"` (Regular) is locked.
   - When users type into standard placeholders, PowerPoint automatically applies the approved weight (e.g., Bold for Montserrat Title, Regular for Roboto Body) without requiring manual formatting.
2. **Embedded TrueType Font Subsets (`<p:embeddedFontLst>`)**:
   - Only the specific approved font weights (`Montserrat-Bold.ttf`, `Montserrat-Regular.ttf`, `BarlowCondensed-Bold.ttf`, `Roboto-Regular.ttf`, `Roboto-Bold.ttf`) are embedded within the `.potx` package under `ppt/fonts/`.
   - On recipient machines that do not have the complete font family installed (which may include unauthorized weights like Thin, ExtraLight, Black), non-approved weights cannot be rendered or selected. PowerPoint resolves text strictly against the embedded TrueType binaries.
3. **Reset Layout Protection**:
   - When a user applies ad-hoc or unauthorized direct formatting overrides (such as making a title Light or Italic), clicking **"Reset Layout"** in PowerPoint instantly strips local overrides and snaps the text back to the placeholder’s master-defined weight and geometry.
4. **Style Guide Boundary**:
   - Because the Office GUI font dropdown enumerates all font weights installed in the local Windows OS font cache (which cannot be hidden by an XML template alone), the template enforces default typing behavior and embedded rendering, while the **Brand Style Guide** establishes the governance rule specifying approved numerical weights (Bold 700, SemiBold 600, Medium 500, Regular 400).
