## Slide 2: Pain relief patches are the most under-built brand category in Indian consumer health

**$3.5Bn** — India pain management market today — still ~60% pills

**$50Mn+** — Patches segment, growing fast off a tiny base

**High-margin, low competition** — Online-first, few organized brands vs. mature markets (US, SE Asia)

**The white space**
Indians are wary of long-term pill use but distrust gels and physiotherapy is expensive and low-awareness. Patches solve for efficacy without the perceived baggage: no mess, targeted relief, culturally acceptable for repeat use. Herbal formulations widen this further — Viopatch is the category's earliest and most established example.

## Slide 3: Viopatch is a category-defining brand

**1. India's first herbal patch**
5 pain-relieving actives (wintergreen, menthol, camphor, eucalyptus, clove) delivering 12-hour relief — built as a category, not a single SKU.

**2. Clinically proven**
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial: pain onset relief at ~13 min, statistically significant vs. placebo. Independently tested for skin safety.

**3. Certified & export-ready**
AYUSH Premium Mark, ISO 9001/45001/13485, CE. ANVISA-approved for Brazil — the only Asian facility with that approval. Exported to 18 countries.

**4. A brand, not just a product**
Line extensions already proven: period-pain patches, headache-cooling patches, mosquito patches, sleep patches — a platform for a modern pain & wellness house.

## Slide 7(or 8): Why this fits a brand-incubation thesis

**A brand with the hard parts already solved**
Formulation, clinical validation, manufacturing relationship, and regulatory approvals exist today — acquisition buys years of R&D and compliance, not a concept.

**A recurring-demand category, not a fad**
Pain management is daily and inescapable across chronic, recurring, and acute use-cases — the opposite of a hit-driven consumer bet.

**Clear value-creation levers post-acquisition**
Sharper D2C/q-commerce positioning, persona-based line extension, and subscription mechanics — all still under-exploited by the current owner.
